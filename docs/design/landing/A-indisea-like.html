03/what it is

TendAI Custody moves medical records between clinics, hospitals and apps only after the patient approves each share. The patient sees who has what and can revoke access at any time.

Before approving, the patient reads a summary made in a NEAR AI Cloud TEE. Every approval, open and revoke lands in a custody log on NEAR.

04/the problem

Records shouldn'ttravel by fax orby guesswork

Referrals still run on faxes, portals and phone calls. Patients cannot see who received what, and clinics cannot tell which copy is current.

Revoking is the hard part. TendAI Custody stops new access right away. It cannot erase a copy someone already downloaded, and it says so up front.

05/how it works

A relay between the patient and every place a record has to go

The patient picks the sections and a recipient. The recipient gets an expiring, ticketed link, and the approval is logged on NEAR.

App copies are de-identified on a best-effort basis. The patient sees a preview before anything is sent.

06/three moments
Moment 1

A referral to a specialist

The front desk asks, the patient approves on a phone, and the specialist opens the record for a short window. No fax, no CD.

Moment 2

A second-opinion share

Send imaging and notes to another doctor. The patient chooses what goes, reads the private summary first, and can stop it later.

Moment 3

Changing your mind

Revoke and new opens are blocked. A copy that was already downloaded stays with its holder, and the log shows that too.

07/who it is for

Built for clinic staff first. TendAI Custody handles the sharing layer so front desks and care teams stay on care, not paperwork.
