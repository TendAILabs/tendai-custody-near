01How this works

Three roles. Maya stays in control.

This is a short walk through a fictional referral. You can follow the guided demo, or look around on your own.

01 · Clinic A

The clinic sends the referral

Clinic A offers Maya’s chart to the hospital. Only ids, hashes, and times are written to NEAR. Her health record stays off the chain.

02 · Maya

Maya reviews and approves

Maya reads a sealed summary, leaves out anything the hospital does not need, then approves the share herself.

03 · Hospital B

The hospital can open the chart

After she approves, Hospital B can open the referral. That open is logged, so Maya can see who looked and when.
