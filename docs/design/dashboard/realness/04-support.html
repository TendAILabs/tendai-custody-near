10Privacy notice

How this demo treats information

Plain language for a first-time visitor. Designed around HIPAA principles, not certified.

01 · Fictional data only

Maya, the clinic, the hospital, and every chart in this demo are fictional. Do not enter a real patient record, a real name, or a real contact detail that you would not want shown in a public demo.

02 · What is stored on NEAR

The contract stores ids, hashes, and timestamps: who shared what, with whom, and when. It does not store health data, names, or the text of a chart.

03 · Sealed summaries

A short summary is written inside a NEAR AI Cloud TEE, a locked server that even we cannot read. Maya can read that summary before she approves a share.

04 · De-identification is best effort

A stripped copy removes common identifiers and shows a preview first. This may miss something. It is not a promise that the copy cannot be re-identified.

05 · Revoking access

Revoking stops the next open. It cannot erase a copy someone already downloaded or saved.

06 · This support form

The contact form in this demo does not email anyone and does not store your message. In a live product, a real form would say how long a message is kept and who can read it.

10Terms of use

A practice demo on testnet

Please read this before you click around. It is short on purpose.

01 · What this is

TendAI Custody is a hackathon demo that runs on NEAR testnet. It shows how a patient-approved referral and a custody log can work. It is not a live clinic system and not a medical device.

02 · What this is not

The demo is designed around HIPAA principles.

Do not load a real patient record into this product.

03 · Testnet, not mainnet

Actions you see here are on a practice network. They are not mainnet transactions and not real payments.

04 · No professional advice

The fictional chart, the sealed summary, and the hospital reply are examples only. They are not medical advice.

05 · Your use

You may explore the guided demo, the log, Help, and Support. Do not try to load real health information, and do not treat the form as a live support channel.
