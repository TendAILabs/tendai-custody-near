Questions a first visit often raises

Short answers in plain language. The story, the clinic, and every chart you see here are fictional.

Question 01 of 10

Who can see Maya’s records?

Only the people she approves. Clinic A already holds the source chart, because that is where the story starts.

Hospital B can open the referral after Maya approves it. Dr. Reyes sees a share only if Maya sends one. PulseFit gets a stripped copy only if she sends one.

Nobody else in this demo can open her chart.

Question 02 of 10

What does Revoke do?

Revoke stops that holder from opening the chart again. The stop is written to the log on NEAR, so Maya can see that it happened.

In the example story, Maya revokes St. Aster General. The hospital’s next open is refused and logged.

Question 03 of 10

What can Revoke not do?

Revoke cannot erase a copy someone already downloaded or saved. Those copies stay where they are.

This is an honest limit. We do not claim that revoking reaches into another person’s device and deletes a file.

Question 04 of 10

What is stored on NEAR?

Ids, hashes, and timestamps. Who shared what, with whom, and when.

No health data, names, or chart text are written to the chain. The record itself stays off NEAR. You can read the same events in plain words on the custody log.

Question 05 of 10

What does the sealed AI do?

NEAR AI Cloud TEE writes a short summary inside a locked server that even we cannot read. That is the sealed AI.

Maya reads that summary before she approves. A signature called provider_tee proves the summary came from that sealed server. Approve stays off unless that mark is present.

Question 06 of 10

How does de-identification work, and what are its limits?

It removes common identifiers, such as name, date of birth, record number, address, phone, and calendar dates. Absolute dates become Day 0 or Day +14. Maya sees a preview before anything is sent.

This is best effort. It may miss something, so she should read the preview carefully. We do not claim the copy is impossible to re-identify.
