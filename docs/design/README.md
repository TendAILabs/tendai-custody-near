# Design references

These are the locked mockups the app was built from. Placeholder times, hashes, and transaction ids in the HTML are made up. The live app shows testnet data only.

## Locked

- `landing/A-indisea-like.html` is the locked landing design, with the A2 polish. It is live at `/landing`.
- `dashboard/C2.html` is the locked dashboard. `dashboard/AUDIT.md` is the rationale. Shipped in the C2 port.
- `dashboard/realness/` holds the five locked realness screens: intro, guided demo, Help, Support, and the plain custody log, plus `shell.css`.

## Fonts

`landing/fonts/` and `dashboard/fonts/` are IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono, with the SIL Open Font License in `LICENSE-ibm-plex-sans.txt` and `LICENSE-ibm-plex-mono.txt`.

## UI rules

- No fully rounded pill buttons.
- No thick outlines on small pills.
- IBM Plex only.
- The dashboard matches the landing.
- A highlight block must not touch the line of text above it.
